5-week solo school project

<!--more-->

Time: 5 weeks  
Team: solo project  
Engine: Unity  

Agility dog! was my first semester exam project. This game was my take on the "platformer" theme we were given; the goal is for the player to get to the end of the course with the shortest time.  
Since this was an individual project, and I was still very new to making games, I tried to keep the style of this prototype very simple. I wanted the UI to match the playful and silly gameplay, and have it look as if the dog character is "saying" or "thinking" the different UI components.
