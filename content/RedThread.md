## Game artwork

### Miksturselskapet

Go to Miksturselskapet's [project page](/games/Dragepust)  
A 5-week group project, where I worked on level design and UI (Unreal).  
You play an alchemist's apprentice being tasked with taking over the shop while she is away on a mission. Through your choices and potion-making skills, you can either help or hinder the townsfolk dealing with the consquences of the infectious Dragepust spreading throughout the world.  
Using the backdrop of the TTRPG Dragepust's worldbuilding and our graphics team's visual inspiration for our game, I came up with a norse and folklore-inspired style for the UI, using illuminated scripts as a reference. The objects  and style used for the UI help reinforce the gameplay story driven potion making.  
![Main menu screen](/games/Dragepust/dragepust-mainmenu.png)

![example book pages](/games/Dragepust/book-s1.png)

### Agility dog!

Go to Agility Dog's [project page](/games/doggo)  
A 5-week solo project (Unity)  
This prototype was a spin on the platformer theme we were given for this assignment. You play a very good dog trying to get the best time on each obstacle course. Because the dog's movement is physics based, the race can end up being pretty silly. I wanted to mirror that silliness in the style of UI for the game.  
![Start screen main menu](/games/agility-dog/doggo-mainmenu.png)

![Controls pop-up from pause menu](/games/agility-dog/doggo-pause2.png)

### Blog game jam

Go to Blog game jam's [project page](/games/blog)  
A 4-day group game jam. I made UI assets (Unity)  
You play "yourself" (a college-aged girl probably in the early 2000's) who gets sent a cute desktop pet program from a friend. The desktop friend just wants to help make your online life the best it can be, by taking it over for you! We all were inspired by the tech and style of the late 90's early 2000's, so I used an alternative color palette when making the Windows XP-like UI. I used a round and friendly dolphin as a callback to the Office Assisstants, but it devolves into a sharp-angled goblin shark as it takes over your blog and PC.   
![chat, start menu open, desktop pet](/games/Desktop/blog2.png)

![overview of all assets I made](/games/Desktop/www-UI-summary.png)

## Illustrations

### Hajk

Go to Hajk's [project page](/illustrations/hajk)  
Hajk was a group project at HDK, where we would pitch a design or product to a client to help promote camping in Sweden. We built this concept as a rental and guide system to help make starting the process of going out into nature easier. One main aspect was a guidebook, that would have useful info, facts, maps, etc and were location-specific. They could be kept for future outings and as collectibles to show where you have gone. I did some of the informational illustrations in our mockup guidebook, as well as graphics, and gifs or videos for our powerpoint presentations.  
![backpack illustration](/illustration/hajk/backpack.jpg)

![tent illustration](/illustration/hajk/tent.jpg)

### Tamabi posters

Go to Tamabi posters' [project page](/illustrations/tamabipostermain)  
During my exchange semester I took an illustration class that focused on a poster series with set themes for each. The first was Earthquake, which is rather relevant to Japan. Instead of dwelling on the negative aspects of earthquakes, I researched and illustrated mythological explanations for what causes earthquakes according to different cultures. 
![Earthquake theme](/illustration/tamabiposters/earthquakeposter.png)

### Plant zine

Go to Plant zine's [project page](/illustrations/plantzine)
