outdoors rental service group project

<!--more-->

As a group project for Swedish Camping as a main client, Hajk is an outdoor equipment rental and inspiration service concept. Through an app the customer can book different camping packages, get sent home a booklet with information and inspiration for camping in their desired area, pick up and return camping gear etc. The main goal of the service is to get customers to more easily out into nature, by simplifying the initial planning and packing stage as well as the need to own the gear if camping is not a frequent activity.  
One main aspect was the guidebook, that would have useful info, facts, maps, etc and were location-specific. They could be kept for future outings and as collectibles to show where you have gone.  
I did some of the informational illustrations in our mockup guidebook, as well as graphics, gifs and videos for our powerpoint presentations.
