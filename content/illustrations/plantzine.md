a fanzine style project from HDK

<!--more-->

 This project was a way for me to learn about illustration, graphics design, and printing techniques. the final product was a short zine containing beginner friendly information about how to take care of house plants, printed on an A2 sized paper and folded up to A5. all text is printed on one side, and a poster illustration is on the other. All illustrations were done with watercolor.
