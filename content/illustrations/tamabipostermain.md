A poster illustration class from Tama Art University, where every year they work with the same themes.
<!--more-->

Every year in this illustration class the students make posters based on the same themes and some set requirements: First is Earthquake, second is Self, third is Societal Problem. For the Earthquake poster I decided to illustrate different folk/mythological reasonings for what causes earthquakes, made digitally.  
The Self poster is made to mimic a playing card with different aspects of myself, painted with watercolor and digitally edited.  
The Societal problem is one from personal experience, the pressure girls have to conform to certain, often contradicting, "rules", painted with ink and digitally edited.
