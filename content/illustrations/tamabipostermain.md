A poster illustration class from Tama Art University, where every year they work with the same themes.
<!--more-->

Every year in the poster class the student make posters based on the same themes and some set requirements: First is Earthquake, second is Self, third is Societal Problem. For the Earthquake poster I decided to illustrate different folk/mythological reasonings for what causes earthquakes. The Self poster is made to mimic a playing card with different aspects of myself. The Societal problem is one from personal experience, the pressure girls have to be a certain, often contradicting way.
