Final Project from Folkuniversitets Art College

<!--more-->

My final project at Art College was learning to make a comic strip series, based on my experiences moving to Sweden from the USA. I researched a lot of Calvin and Hobbes because I liked the joke structure and style of Bill Watterson's comic. I made a small booklet and presented it as well as process documentation as my final presentation. These are a few of the strips I still like.
