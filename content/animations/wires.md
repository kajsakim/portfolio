An animation short following the premise "one sentence animation", where the whole plot is one sentence long.

<!--more-->

This short was my main project while on an exchange semester to Tama Art University in Tokyo. It was set up in a production-pipeline manner, with plot and character design first, then storyboards and animatic, then final animation and sound. My one sentence plot was "A deer girl discovers a factory monster in the woods", where she digs up wires in her garden that lead back to an abandoned factory that has been taken over by the factory "monster".
