graphics for Hurley Classic event

<!--more-->

These are maps made for the Hurley Classic in 2019, a yearly whitewater kayaking event in the UK (now discontinued). I needed to make them with their established graphic profile in mind, and to be used online and in print at the event. These two maps are race events with different obstacles along the race path in the river.
