Graphic design for event

<!--more-->

Pawske Party is a yearly event in Oslo on Easter. since 2024 I have been designing the announcement flyers and DJ poster for the event. Sometimes I do illustrations for them, and other times I work with another artist to make art for the poster/flyer. 

![Pawske party DJ lineup poster](pawske-dj26.png)

I made the art in the DJ poster, but not for the flyer here. The event board required at least 3 photos from prior years to be included on the flyer, along with other info requirements.

![Pawske party announcement flyer](pawske-flyer2027.png)
