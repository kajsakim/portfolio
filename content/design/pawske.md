Graphic design for event

<!--more-->

Pawske Party is a yearly dance event in Oslo during Easter. since 2024 I have been designing the announcement flyers and DJ posters for the event. Sometimes I do illustrations for them, and other times I work with another artist to make art for the poster/flyer. Working with another artist can be a challenge, because it requires me to be more flexible in adapting my designs than when I illustrate and design myself, but it often leads to good results.

![Pawske party DJ lineup poster](/design/pawskeParty/pawske-dj26.png)

I made the art in the DJ poster, but not for the flyer here. I have been allowed to use other fonts when making the DJ poster other than the ones required for the event flyers, which I think really helps make the DJ posters feel more dynamic and higher energy.

For this flyer, the event board required at least 3 photos from prior years to be included on the flyer, along with specific event info, the QR code and logo. The board told me after delivery that they felt this was the best flyer to date, which feels nice to hear!
